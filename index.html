What a gourd can tell

Thirty-one scenes fit on the surface of a gourd. I follow the story carved into it and the path the object took from Peru to Germany.

Material culture · Provenance

Between the Andes and the Alps

I trace the journey of a gourd carved by Guillermo Ventura. Johanna Schupp bought it in Cochas Chico in 1983, brought it to Borken in 1987, and donated it to the BASA Museum in 2021. Cataloged as Schu18, the gourd depicts a child’s illness, diagnosis, and recovery in 31 scenes set in the Mantaro Valley.

Ethnography · Peru

Mantaro Valley

My doctoral research examines Wanka identity through two practices in Peru’s Mantaro Valley: gourd carving in Cochas Chico and the pagapu offering ritual at Huaytapallana.

What museums can show

Online exhibition · BASA Museum · 2026

Landscapes in Transformation. The Cochabamba Valley Beyond the Inkas

I created the online exhibition in ArcGIS StoryMaps and translated all exhibition texts into German and English. The exhibition brings together objects from the Bonn Collection of the Americas, colonial sources, and local memories to show how different societies have shaped the Cochabamba Valley.

About Joaquín

I am a doctoral candidate in Anthropology of the Americas at the University of Bonn. At the University of Chile, I earned a bachelor’s degree in Hispanic Language and Literature and a master’s degree in Art, specializing in Art Theory and History.
